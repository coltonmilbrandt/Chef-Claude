# Chicken Taco Bowls

**Source:** [Chicken Taco Poblano Rice Bowls](https://www.skinnytaste.com/chicken-taco-poblano-rice-bowls/)
**Prep Time:** 10 min | **Cook Time:** 15 min | **Total:** 25 min
**Servings:** 2

## Description
Seasoned chicken with poblano peppers and corn over rice, topped with fresh pico de gallo. Light, healthy, and packed with flavor.

## Ingredients

### Main
- 10 oz boneless skinless chicken breast, cut into 1/2-inch cubes
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 poblano pepper, seeded and diced
- 1 roma tomato, diced
- 1/2 cup frozen corn kernels
- 1.5 cups cooked rice
- 2 tablespoons shredded cheddar-jack cheese

### Taco Seasoning
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon chili powder
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon oregano
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper (extra heat)

### Pico de Gallo
- 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro, minced
- 1 tablespoon lime juice (about 1/2 lime)
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- Reserved diced tomato

### For Serving
- 1 lime, cut into wedges
- Sriracha or hot sauce
- Sour cream or greek yogurt (optional)

## Instructions

1. **Season chicken:** Mix all taco seasoning ingredients in a small bowl. Dice chicken and toss with seasoning.
2. **Make pico:** Combine diced tomato, cilantro, lime juice, and salt in a small bowl. Set aside.
3. **Cook chicken:** Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken and cook 2-3 minutes until starting to brown.
4. **Add veggies:** Add poblano pepper and taco seasoning. Cook 2-3 minutes until softened. Add 1/4 cup water and corn. Cook 1-2 minutes more.
5. **Assemble bowls:** Divide rice between 2 bowls. Top with chicken mixture, pico de gallo, cheese, and lime wedges.

## Tips
- Jalapeño can be added to the pico for more heat.
- Cauliflower rice works great as a low-carb swap.
- Leftover chicken keeps 3 days in the fridge — great for next-day lunch.
