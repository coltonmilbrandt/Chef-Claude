# Spicy Chicken & Bell Pepper Stir Fry

**Source:** [Chicken Peppers Stir Fry](https://www.eatingonadime.com/chicken-peppers-stir-fry-recipe/)
**Prep Time:** 10 min | **Cook Time:** 15 min | **Total:** 25 min
**Servings:** 2

## Description
A quick and fiery chicken stir fry with colorful bell peppers in a savory ginger-garlic sauce. Served over rice, ready in 25 minutes.

## Ingredients

### Main
- 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breast, sliced thin
- 2 bell peppers (any color), sliced
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil

### Sauce
- 3 tablespoons low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 1/2 cup chicken broth
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 1 tablespoon sriracha (or more to taste)
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, minced

### For Serving
- 1.5 cups cooked rice
- Sesame seeds
- Sliced green onions (optional)

## Instructions

1. **Make the sauce:** Whisk together soy sauce, cornstarch, chicken broth, honey, and sriracha in a small bowl. Set aside.
2. **Cook chicken:** Heat oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add chicken slices, season with salt and pepper. Cook 4-5 minutes until browned and cooked through. Remove to a plate.
3. **Cook peppers:** In the same skillet, add bell peppers. Stir fry 2-3 minutes until tender-crisp.
4. **Add aromatics:** Add garlic and ginger, cook 1 minute until fragrant.
5. **Combine:** Return chicken to the skillet. Pour sauce over everything. Stir and cook 1-2 minutes until sauce thickens and coats everything.
6. **Serve:** Spoon over rice. Top with sesame seeds and green onions if desired.

## Tips
- Slice chicken thin for faster cooking and better sauce absorption.
- Add red pepper flakes or extra sriracha for more heat.
- Broccoli florets or snap peas can be added with the bell peppers.
