# Spicy Thai Chicken Lettuce Wraps

**Source:** [Thai Chicken Lettuce Wraps](https://www.apinchofhealthy.com/thai-chicken-lettuce-wraps/)
**Prep Time:** 5 min | **Cook Time:** 10 min | **Total:** 15 min
**Servings:** 2

## Description
Ground chicken in a spicy Thai sauce with cashews, carrots, and fresh herbs, wrapped in crisp butter lettuce leaves. Ultra-light and ready in 15 minutes.

## Ingredients

### Main
- 10 oz ground chicken
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, minced
- 1/3 cup shredded carrots
- 1/4 cup cashews, chopped
- 1 head butter lettuce, leaves separated

### Sauce
- 2 tablespoons low-sodium soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons Thai sweet red chili sauce
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1 teaspoon honey
- 1 teaspoon sriracha (or more to taste)

### Toppings
- 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro, chopped
- 2 tablespoons green onions, sliced
- Sesame seeds
- Extra sriracha

## Instructions

1. **Cook aromatics:** Heat sesame oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add garlic and ginger, cook 1 minute until fragrant.
2. **Brown chicken:** Add ground chicken, breaking it apart. Season with salt and pepper. Cook 3-4 minutes until golden brown.
3. **Make sauce:** In a small bowl, whisk together soy sauce, chili sauce, lime juice, honey, and sriracha.
4. **Combine:** Pour sauce over chicken. Add cashews. Cook 2 minutes on low heat, stirring to coat.
5. **Finish:** Remove from heat. Stir in shredded carrots, green onions, and cilantro.
6. **Serve:** Spoon chicken mixture into butter lettuce leaves. Top with sesame seeds and extra sriracha.

## Tips
- Use chicken breast instead of ground chicken — just dice it small.
- These are great for meal prep — keep filling and lettuce separate until serving.
- Add Thai bird chiles or red pepper flakes for serious heat.
