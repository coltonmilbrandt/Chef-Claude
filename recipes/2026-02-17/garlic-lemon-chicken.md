# Garlic Lemon Chicken with Veggies

**Source:** [20-Minute Pan-Fried Lemon Garlic Chicken](https://tiffycooks.com/20-minute-pan-fried-lemon-garlic-chicken/)
**Prep Time:** 5 min | **Cook Time:** 15 min | **Total:** 20 min
**Servings:** 2

## Description
Crispy pan-fried chicken thighs in a bright lemon-garlic sauce with zucchini and bell peppers. Simple, flavorful, and done in 20 minutes.

## Ingredients

### Main
- 4 boneless skinless chicken thighs (~1 lb), cut into 1.5-inch pieces
- 1 bell pepper, sliced
- 1 medium zucchini, sliced into half-moons
- 6-8 cloves garlic, roughly chopped
- 1 lemon, half juiced + half sliced
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- Fresh cilantro for garnish

### Marinade/Sauce
- 1.5 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- Black pepper to taste

### For Serving
- 1.5 cups cooked rice (optional)
- Red pepper flakes

## Instructions

1. **Marinate chicken:** Combine chicken pieces with soy sauce, honey, cornstarch, and black pepper. Let rest 5 minutes while prepping veggies.
2. **Pan fry chicken:** Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken and cook 6-7 minutes until browned on all sides.
3. **Cook veggies:** Push chicken to one side. Add bell pepper and zucchini. Cook 2-3 minutes until slightly tender.
4. **Add garlic:** Add chopped garlic, cook 1 minute until fragrant.
5. **Finish:** Squeeze lemon juice over everything. Add lemon slices to the pan. Toss to combine. Cook 1 minute more.
6. **Serve:** Plate over rice if desired. Garnish with cilantro and red pepper flakes.

## Tips
- Chicken thighs stay juicier than breasts in this recipe, but either works.
- The cornstarch in the marinade creates a light crispy coating.
- Add jalapeño slices with the veggies for extra heat.
