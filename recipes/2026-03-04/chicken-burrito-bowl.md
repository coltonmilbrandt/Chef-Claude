# Spicy Chicken Burrito Bowl

**Source:** [Chicken Burrito Bowl Meal Prep - Damn Delicious](https://damndelicious.net/2017/05/12/chicken-burrito-bowl-meal-prep/)
**Prep Time:** 10 min | **Cook Time:** 15 min | **Total:** 25 min
**Servings:** 2

## Description
A loaded burrito bowl with seasoned chicken, black beans, corn, and a spicy chipotle cream sauce. Quick, healthy, and packed with flavor.

## Ingredients

### Main
- 3/4 lb chicken breast, diced into small pieces
- 1 (15 oz) can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1/2 (15 oz) can corn, drained (other half used in tacos)
- Leftover rice from curry night (or cook 1/2 cup fresh)
- 1 jalapeño, diced
- 1/2 tbsp olive oil

### Seasonings
- 1/4 tsp chili powder
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp cumin
- 1/4 tsp oregano
- 1/8 tsp paprika
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Chipotle Cream Sauce
- 1/4 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
- 1 chipotle pepper in adobo sauce, minced
- 1/2 clove garlic, minced
- 1/2 tbsp fresh lime juice

### For Serving
- 2 tbsp pico de gallo or diced fresh tomatoes
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh cilantro
- 1/2 avocado, sliced
- Lime wedges

## Instructions

1. **Make sauce:** Mix Greek yogurt, minced chipotle pepper, garlic, and lime juice in a small bowl. Set aside.
2. **Cook chicken:** Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Season diced chicken with all spices. Cook 5-7 minutes until browned and cooked through.
3. **Heat beans & corn:** Add black beans, corn, and diced jalapeño to the skillet. Cook 2-3 minutes until heated through.
4. **Assemble bowls:** Divide rice into 2 bowls. Top with chicken-bean mixture, pico de gallo, avocado, and cilantro.
5. **Drizzle:** Top with chipotle cream sauce and serve with lime wedges.

## Tips
- The chipotle cream sauce is the star — smoky, spicy, tangy.
- Add extra chipotle peppers for more heat.
- Fresh tomatoes work great instead of store-bought pico (Alayha loves them!).
- Jalapeño adds nice fresh heat on top of the chipotle.
