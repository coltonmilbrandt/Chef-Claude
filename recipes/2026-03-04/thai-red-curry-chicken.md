# Thai Red Curry Chicken with Potatoes & Carrots

**Source:** [Thai Red Curry with Chicken - RecipeTin Eats](https://www.recipetineats.com/thai-red-curry-with-chicken/) (adapted with potatoes & carrots)
**Prep Time:** 10 min | **Cook Time:** 20 min | **Total:** 30 min
**Servings:** 2

## Description
A rich, spicy Thai red curry with tender chicken, potatoes, and carrots in a creamy coconut sauce. Hearty, warming, and served over jasmine rice.

## Ingredients

### Main
- 3/4 lb chicken thighs, sliced thin
- 1 medium potato, diced small (~1/2 inch cubes)
- 2 carrots, sliced into rounds
- 1 (13.5 oz) can full-fat coconut milk
- 1/2 cup chicken broth
- 3/4 cup jasmine rice

### Aromatics
- 1 shallot, minced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 1/2 tbsp vegetable oil

### Seasonings
- 2-3 tbsp red curry paste (more = spicier)
- 1 tsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp sugar
- Salt to taste

### For Serving
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh cilantro
- 1 tbsp fresh lime juice
- Fresh red chili slices (optional, for extra heat)

## Instructions

1. **Cook rice:** Cook jasmine rice according to package directions. Make extra for stir fry and burrito bowl later in the week.
2. **Bloom curry paste:** Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add curry paste and cook 2 minutes, stirring, until fragrant and slightly darkened.
3. **Sauté aromatics:** Add shallot, garlic, and ginger. Cook 1 minute.
4. **Build sauce:** Pour in chicken broth and stir. Simmer 2 minutes until reduced slightly. Add coconut milk, fish sauce, and sugar. Stir to combine.
5. **Cook chicken & veggies:** Add sliced chicken, diced potato, and carrot rounds. Bring to a simmer. Cook 12-15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until potatoes are fork-tender and chicken is cooked through.
6. **Finish:** Remove from heat. Stir in lime juice. Taste and adjust salt/fish sauce.
7. **Serve:** Ladle over rice. Garnish with cilantro and fresh chili slices.

## Tips
- Dice potatoes small (1/2 inch) so they cook quickly.
- The potatoes thicken the sauce beautifully as they cook.
- Make a double batch of rice — reuse for stir fry and burrito bowl.
- Uses shallot instead of onion (Colton-approved).
- Add more curry paste for serious heat.
