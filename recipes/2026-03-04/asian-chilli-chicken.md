# Asian Chilli Chicken

**Source:** [Asian Chilli Chicken - RecipeTin Eats](https://www.recipetineats.com/asian-chilli-chicken/)
**Prep Time:** 5 min | **Cook Time:** 8 min | **Total:** 13 min
**Servings:** 2

## Description
Ultra-quick spicy chicken with a sticky sriracha-lime-honey glaze. Served over rice with green onions and sesame seeds.

## Ingredients

### Main
- 1/2 lb chicken breast, sliced into thin steaks
- 1 bell pepper, sliced (side veggie)
- Leftover rice from curry night (or cook 3/4 cup fresh)

### Sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 clove garlic, finely minced
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, finely minced
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes
- 1/4 cup water
- 1 1/2 tbsp sriracha
- 1/2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 1/2 tbsp fresh lime juice

### Coating
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp pepper
- 1 tbsp rice flour (or all-purpose flour)
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil

### For Serving
- 1 green onion, chopped
- Sesame seeds
- Lime wedges

## Instructions

1. **Prep chicken:** Cut chicken breast horizontally into thin steaks. Season both sides with salt, pepper, and rice flour.
2. **Sear chicken:** Heat oil in a large skillet over high heat. Cook chicken 2 minutes per side until golden. Remove and set aside.
3. **Make sauce:** Reduce heat slightly. Add sesame oil, garlic, and ginger. Cook 15 seconds. Add chili flakes, cook 30 seconds.
4. **Build glaze:** Add water, sriracha, soy sauce, and honey. Stir and simmer 2 minutes until thickened.
5. **Finish:** Add lime juice, simmer 30 seconds. Return chicken to pan, coat with sauce.
6. **Stir fry bell pepper:** In a separate pan, quickly stir fry sliced bell pepper with a splash of soy sauce (2-3 min).
7. **Serve:** Plate over rice with stir-fried bell pepper. Top with green onion, sesame seeds, and lime wedges.

## Tips
- Fastest dinner of the week — 13 minutes!
- Have all ingredients prepped before you start; it moves fast.
- Add more chili flakes or fresh Thai chilis for extra heat.
