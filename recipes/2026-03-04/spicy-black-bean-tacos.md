# Spicy Black Bean Tacos

**Source:** [Quinoa Black Bean Tacos - Damn Delicious](https://damndelicious.net/2014/04/19/quinoa-black-bean-tacos/) (simplified — skip quinoa)
**Prep Time:** 5 min | **Cook Time:** 10 min | **Total:** 15 min
**Servings:** 2

## Description
Quick vegetarian tacos with spiced black beans, diced tomatoes & green chiles, fresh avocado, cilantro, and lime. Healthy, filling, and fast.

## Ingredients

### Main
- 1 (15 oz) can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 (10 oz) can diced tomatoes & green chiles (Rotel), undrained
- 1/2 cup frozen corn kernels
- 4 small flour or corn tortillas
- 1/2 avocado, diced

### Seasonings
- 1/2 tsp chili powder
- 1/4 tsp cumin
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder
- 1/8 tsp cayenne pepper
- Salt and pepper to taste

### For Serving
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh cilantro
- 1/2 lime, juiced
- Hot sauce to taste

## Instructions

1. **Heat beans:** In a skillet over medium heat, combine black beans, diced tomatoes & green chiles, and corn. Stir in chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, and cayenne.
2. **Simmer:** Cook 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thickened and heated through. Season with salt and pepper.
3. **Finish filling:** Stir in lime juice and cilantro.
4. **Warm tortillas:** Heat tortillas in a dry skillet or microwave.
5. **Assemble:** Spoon bean mixture into tortillas. Top with diced avocado and extra hot sauce.

## Tips
- Simplified from original recipe — skipped quinoa to keep it under 15 min.
- The Rotel adds plenty of heat, but add more cayenne or jalapeño for extra spice.
- Leftover bean mixture works great over rice the next day.
- The one meatless meal of the week — light and satisfying.
