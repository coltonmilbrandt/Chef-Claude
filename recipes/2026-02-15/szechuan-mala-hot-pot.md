# Szechuan Mala Hot Pot with Noodles

**Source:** [The Woks of Life - Hot Pot](https://thewoksoflife.com/hot-pot/)
**Prep Time:** 20 min | **Cook Time:** 15 min (broth) + cook-at-table | **Total:** ~1 hour experience
**Servings:** 2

## Description
A fiery, numbing Szechuan-style hot pot using a store-bought mala soup base for maximum flavor with minimum effort. Cook thinly sliced chicken, noodles, veggies, and tofu right at the table in a bubbling, tongue-tingling broth. Perfect for two people who LOVE spicy food.

## Ingredients

### Broth
- 1 package spicy mala hot pot soup base (Haidilao or Little Sheep spicy)
- 6-8 cups chicken broth (2 boxes, 32 oz each)
- 4 cloves garlic, smashed
- 4 slices fresh ginger
- 2 tablespoons Szechuan peppercorns (ALREADY HAVE - toast before adding)
- 8-10 dried red chilies (extra spicy kick on top of the mala base)

### Protein
- 1 lb thinly sliced beef (sandwich-style shaved beef, ~$5-6 — already thin, no extra slicing needed. If unavailable, sub chicken breast sliced thin)

### Noodles & Starches
- 2 packages dried ramen noodles (discard seasoning packets)
- 1 package glass noodles (mung bean vermicelli), ~3.5 oz

### Vegetables
- 1 small head napa cabbage, cut into chunks
- 1 bunch baby bok choy, halved

### Tofu
- 1 block firm tofu, cut into 1-inch cubes

### Dipping Sauce
- 3 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
- 1 tablespoon chili garlic sauce (Huy Fong)
- 2 green onions, thinly sliced
- Fresh cilantro, chopped

## Instructions

1. **Prep everything first:** Separate beef slices and arrange on a plate (already thin — no extra slicing needed). Cut napa cabbage into chunks, halve bok choy, cube tofu. Arrange everything on plates/platters.

2. **Soak glass noodles:** Place glass noodles in a bowl of hot water for 10 minutes until softened. Drain.

3. **Toast peppercorns:** In a dry pan over medium heat, toast Szechuan peppercorns for 1-2 minutes until fragrant and slightly darkened. Set aside.

4. **Build the broth:** In a large pot or hot pot vessel, combine the mala soup base packet with chicken broth. Add smashed garlic, ginger slices, toasted Szechuan peppercorns, and dried red chilies. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer for 10 minutes to let flavors meld.

5. **Mix dipping sauce:** Combine soy sauce, sesame oil, rice vinegar, and chili garlic sauce. Top with sliced green onions and cilantro. Each person can adjust to taste.

6. **Cook at the table:** Transfer broth to your hot pot setup (or keep on stove). Maintain a gentle boil. Add items in batches:
   - **Beef:** 30-60 seconds (shaved beef cooks super fast — don't overcook!)
   - **Napa cabbage & bok choy:** 2-3 minutes
   - **Tofu:** 2-3 minutes (just to heat through)
   - **Ramen noodles:** 3-4 minutes
   - **Glass noodles:** 1-2 minutes

7. **Eat as you go:** Fish items out, dip in sauce, enjoy. Add more items as you eat. Top off with extra broth or hot water as liquid reduces.

## Tips
- The broth gets MORE flavorful as you cook more items in it - save noodles for last to soak up all that goodness
- Shaved beef cooks in seconds — swish it in the broth and pull it right out
- Add more Szechuan peppercorns or dried chilies during cooking if you want even more heat
- Leftover broth makes an incredible base for soup the next day
- If you don't have a hot pot setup, just use your biggest pot on the stove and everyone gathers around
