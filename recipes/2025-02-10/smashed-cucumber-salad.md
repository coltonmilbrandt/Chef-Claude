# Smashed Cucumber Salad (Snack)

**Source:** [Smashed Asian Cucumber Salad - The Woks of Life](https://thewoksoflife.com/smashed-asian-cucumber-salad/)
**Prep Time:** 10 min | **Total:** 10 min
**Servings:** 4

## Description
A refreshing, crunchy Chinese-style cucumber salad with a spicy, garlicky dressing. The smashing technique creates rough edges that soak up all the delicious sauce. Perfect as a healthy snack or side dish.

## Ingredients

### For the Dressing
- 1 tsp salt
- 2.5 tsp sugar
- 2 tsp sesame oil
- 3 tsp soy sauce
- 1.5 tbsp rice vinegar

### For the Salad
- 1-1.5 lbs seedless cucumbers (about 2 English cucumbers)
- 4 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 2 tsp chili garlic sauce (Huy Fong) - more for extra heat!
- 2 tsp toasted sesame seeds
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
- Red pepper flakes (optional, for extra heat)

## Instructions

1. **Make dressing:** Combine salt, sugar, sesame oil, soy sauce, and rice vinegar in a bowl. Stir until sugar and salt dissolve completely.

2. **Smash cucumbers:** Lay a large knife (or rolling pin) flat against each cucumber and press down firmly to crack it. The cucumber should split into rough sections. Repeat along the entire length.

3. **Cut cucumbers:** Slice smashed cucumbers at 45-degree angles into bite-sized pieces.

4. **Optional salting:** For extra crunch, sprinkle cucumber pieces with a pinch of salt and let sit 10 minutes. Drain any liquid.

5. **Combine:** Toss cucumbers with dressing, garlic, and chili garlic sauce. Mix well so dressing gets into all the crevices.

6. **Garnish:** Top with sesame seeds, cilantro, and red pepper flakes if you want more heat.

7. **Serve:** Best served immediately while cold and crunchy. Can refrigerate up to 2 hours.

## Tips
- Smashing is key - the rough edges absorb more dressing than smooth slices
- For MAXIMUM spice: Double the chili garlic sauce and add red pepper flakes
- English cucumbers from Walmart work perfectly
- Keeps in fridge up to 1 day but gets less crunchy
