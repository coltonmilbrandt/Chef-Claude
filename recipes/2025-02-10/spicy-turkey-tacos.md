# Spicy Turkey Tacos

**Source:** [Ground Turkey Tacos - Skinnytaste](https://www.skinnytaste.com/ground-turkey-tacos/)
**Prep Time:** 10 min | **Cook Time:** 20 min | **Total:** 30 min
**Servings:** 4 (2 tacos each)

## Description
Lean, flavorful turkey tacos with homemade seasoning and fresh toppings. A lighter take on taco night that doesn't sacrifice any of the flavor - and we're making them SPICY!

## Ingredients

### For the Meat
- 1 lb 93% lean ground turkey
- 1.5 tbsp taco seasoning (homemade below or store-bought)
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 small yellow onion, minced
- 2 jalapeños, seeded and diced (leave seeds for more heat!)
- 2/3 cup water
- 1/2 cup canned tomato sauce
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (for extra heat)

### Homemade Taco Seasoning (optional)
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1/4 tsp dried oregano
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
- Salt and pepper to taste

### For Assembly
- 8 small corn tortillas (6-inch)
- 1/2 cup shredded cheese (optional)
- 1.5 cups shredded lettuce
- 1 cup pico de gallo or diced fresh tomatoes
- Fresh cilantro, chopped
- Lime wedges
- Hot sauce (Cholula, Valentina, or your favorite)

## Instructions

1. **Prep toppings:** Dice tomatoes, shred lettuce, chop cilantro before cooking so tacos are served hot.

2. **Brown the turkey:** Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add ground turkey and cook, breaking it into small pieces, until no longer pink (about 5 minutes).

3. **Add aromatics:** Stir in onion, garlic, and jalapeños. Cook 2-3 minutes until softened.

4. **Season and simmer:** Add taco seasoning, cayenne, water, and tomato sauce. Stir well to combine. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer 15-20 minutes until sauce thickens and flavors meld. Taste and adjust salt.

5. **Char tortillas:** Heat tortillas directly over gas flame 30 seconds per side, or warm in a dry skillet until pliable.

6. **Assemble:** Divide meat among tortillas (about 1/4 cup each). Top with cheese, lettuce, pico de gallo, cilantro, and a squeeze of lime.

7. **Serve:** With extra hot sauce on the side!

## Tips
- For MAXIMUM heat: Leave jalapeño seeds in and add extra cayenne
- Lean turkey can dry out - don't skip the sauce!
- Char those tortillas - it makes a huge difference
