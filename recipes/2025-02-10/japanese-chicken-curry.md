# Japanese Chicken Curry with Potatoes

**Source:** [Japanese Beef Curry - Just One Cookbook](https://www.justonecookbook.com/japanese-beef-curry/) (adapted for chicken)
**Prep Time:** 15 min | **Cook Time:** 45 min | **Total:** 1 hour
**Servings:** 4 (makes great leftovers)

## Description
A rich, hearty Japanese-style curry with tender chicken, potatoes, and carrots in a thick, savory-sweet curry sauce. Comfort food at its finest, made extra spicy with cayenne!

## Ingredients

### Protein & Vegetables
- 1.25 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast, cut into 1.5-inch cubes
- 2 large yellow onions (about 1.25 lb), thinly sliced
- 2 medium russet potatoes, cut into large chunks
- 2 large carrots, cut diagonally
- 8 oz baby bella/cremini mushrooms, sliced

### Seasonings & Aromatics
- 1/2 tsp fresh ginger, grated
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp all-purpose flour

### Sauce Components
- 2 tbsp unsalted butter, divided
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil, divided
- 1/2 cup water
- 1 tbsp curry powder (regular, from spice aisle)
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 4 cups chicken broth
- 1 tbsp honey (or grate 1/8 apple)
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 box Golden Curry sauce mix, Medium Hot (S&B brand - at Walmart!)
- 1/2 to 1 tsp cayenne pepper (for extra heat!)

### For Serving
- 4 servings cooked long grain white rice
- Pickled jalapeños (optional topping)

## Instructions

1. **Caramelize onions:** Heat half the butter and oil in a large pot over medium heat. Cook onions 20-25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until tender, translucent, and caramelized. Set aside.

2. **Prep vegetables:** Cut potatoes into large chunks and soak in water 15 minutes. Cut carrots diagonally. Slice mushrooms. Grate ginger and mince garlic.

3. **Season chicken:** Lightly season chicken with salt and pepper, then coat with flour.

4. **Sear chicken:** Heat remaining butter and oil in a separate pan over medium-high. Sear chicken in batches, 3-4 minutes per side until golden. Transfer to plate.

5. **Deglaze:** Add water to the pan, scraping up browned bits.

6. **Build sauce:** Return onions to heat. Add garlic, ginger, curry powder, cayenne, and tomato paste; saute 1 minute. Add chicken, juices, and water from deglazing. Cook 3 minutes.

7. **Simmer:** Add carrots, mushrooms, and chicken broth. Cover and bring to boil, then reduce heat and simmer 25 minutes.

8. **Add potatoes:** Drain potatoes and add to pot. Simmer covered 15 minutes until potatoes are tender.

9. **Finish with roux:** Turn off heat. Break up Golden Curry roux and add 1-2 cubes at a time, stirring until dissolved. Simmer on lowest heat 5 minutes until thick. Add more cayenne if you want it spicier!

10. **Serve:** Portion rice on one half of plate, curry on the other.

## Tips
- Golden Curry "Medium Hot" is at Walmart in the Asian aisle - add cayenne to make it spicier
- The honey adds the signature Japanese curry sweetness
- Even better the next day - flavors meld overnight!
- For MAXIMUM heat: Use 1 full tsp cayenne and add sriracha on top
