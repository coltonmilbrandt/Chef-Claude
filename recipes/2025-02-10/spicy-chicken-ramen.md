# Spicy Chicken Ramen

**Source:** [Spicy Chicken Ramen - Chili Pepper Madness](https://www.chilipeppermadness.com/recipes/chicken-ramen/)
**Prep Time:** 5 min | **Cook Time:** 15 min | **Total:** 20 min
**Servings:** 2

## Description
A deeply flavorful, spicy ramen with tender poached chicken in a sriracha and chili-laced broth. Quick enough for a weeknight but tastes like you simmered it for hours.

## Ingredients

### For the Broth
- 5 cups chicken broth
- 2 jalapeños, sliced (keep seeds for heat!)
- 4 garlic cloves, smashed
- 1 thumb-sized knob fresh ginger, peeled and sliced
- 3 tbsp gochujang (use 4 tbsp for extra heat!)
- 1 tbsp chili garlic sauce (Huy Fong)
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- Extra sriracha to taste

### For the Ramen
- 6 oz boneless chicken breast
- 2 packets instant ramen noodles (discard seasoning packets)
- 1 large carrot, thinly sliced
- 6 baby bella mushrooms, thinly sliced (sub for shiitake)
- 1 tsp sesame oil (plus more for vegetables)
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- Salt to taste
- 2 soft-boiled eggs, halved (6-7 min eggs)

### Garnishes
- Green onions, sliced
- Red pepper flakes
- Sesame seeds
- Extra chili garlic sauce

## Instructions

1. **Make broth:** Heat broth in a pot over medium heat. Add jalapeños, garlic, ginger, gochujang, chili garlic sauce, soy sauce, sesame oil, and vinegar. Stir until incorporated and bring to a boil.

2. **Poach chicken:** Add whole chicken breast to broth, reduce heat to simmer for 10 minutes until cooked through. Remove and shred with two forks.

3. **Cook noodles:** Add ramen noodles to broth and cook 3 minutes until soft. Be careful not to overcook - they'll continue cooking in the hot broth.

4. **Prepare vegetables:** While noodles cook, stir-fry sliced carrots in sesame oil for 2-3 minutes. Toss with a splash of sesame oil and vinegar. Do the same with mushrooms.

5. **Assemble:** Distribute noodles between 2 bowls. Top with shredded chicken, vegetables, and soft-boiled egg halves. Pour hot broth evenly into each bowl.

6. **Garnish:** Top with green onions, red pepper flakes, sesame seeds, and extra chili garlic sauce.

## Tips
- For MAXIMUM spice: Use 4+ tbsp gochujang, leave seeds in the jalapeños, pile on chili garlic sauce
- Don't overcook the noodles - they get soggy fast
- Soft-boiled eggs: 6.5 minutes in boiling water, then ice bath
- Use instant ramen noodles but throw away the seasoning packets!
