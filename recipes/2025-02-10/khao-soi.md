# Khao Soi (Thai Coconut Curry Noodle Soup)

**Source:** [Chicken Khao Soi - The Woks of Life](https://thewoksoflife.com/chicken-khao-soi/)
**Prep Time:** 30 min | **Cook Time:** 30 min | **Total:** 1 hour
**Servings:** 4 (scale to 2 by halving)

## Description
A rich, aromatic Northern Thai curry noodle soup with tender chicken in a spicy coconut curry broth, topped with crispy fried noodles and fresh garnishes.

## Ingredients

### For the Khao Soi Paste
- 4 jalapeños, seeded and chopped (keep seeds for extra heat!)
- 2 shallots, roughly chopped
- 6 cloves garlic
- 1-inch piece ginger, peeled and sliced
- 1/4 cup cilantro (stems and leaves)
- Zest of 1 lime
- 2 tsp ground turmeric
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp curry powder

### For the Soup
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast, sliced
- 2 tbsp Thai Kitchen red curry paste
- 4 cups low sodium chicken broth
- 2 tsp brown sugar
- 14 oz unsweetened coconut milk (1 can)
- 2 tbsp soy sauce + 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce (sub for fish sauce)
- 12 oz dried egg noodles or lo mein noodles

### Garnishes
- Thinly sliced shallots
- Lime wedges
- Pickled jalapeños (optional)
- Crispy fried noodles (optional)
- Chopped cilantro
- Sriracha or chili garlic sauce

## Instructions

1. **Make the paste:** Combine all curry paste ingredients in a food processor and pulse until smooth. (Or just mince everything finely by hand.)

2. **Cook chicken:** Heat oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Stir-fry chicken until browned, then remove and set aside. Add the fresh curry paste to the remaining fat and fry 3-5 minutes until fragrant.

3. **Build the broth:** Add Thai Kitchen curry paste, broth, and brown sugar; bring to a boil. Reduce heat to low, then add coconut milk and soy sauce mixture. Return chicken to the pot. Don't let it boil vigorously or the coconut milk may split.

4. **Cook noodles:** Prepare according to package directions while the broth simmers.

5. **Make crispy topping (optional):** Fry a handful of cooked noodles in 350F oil until golden and crispy.

6. **Serve:** Divide cooked noodles among bowls. Top with chicken and broth. Garnish with onion, lime wedges, cilantro, and sriracha.

## Tips
- For MAXIMUM spice: Keep jalapeño seeds in and add extra sriracha
- The soup balances savory, salty, sour, and sweet - adjust soy sauce and lime to taste
- Don't let soup boil after adding coconut milk
