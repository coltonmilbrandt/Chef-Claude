# Pad Kee Mao (Drunken Noodles)

**Source:** [Thai Drunken Noodles - RecipeTin Eats](https://www.recipetineats.com/pad-kee-mao-thai-drunken-noodles/)
**Prep Time:** 10 min | **Cook Time:** 6 min | **Total:** 16 min
**Servings:** 2-3

## Description
A fiery Thai stir-fry with wide rice noodles, tender chicken, and loads of fresh basil. The "drunken" name comes from its reputation as a hangover cure - or because it's so spicy you'll need a cold beer!

## Ingredients

### Noodles
- 8 oz rice stick noodles / pad thai noodles

### Stir Fry
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 3 large garlic cloves, minced
- 3 jalapeños, seeded and finely chopped (keep seeds for extra heat!)
- 1/2 yellow onion, sliced
- 7 oz (200g) boneless chicken breast, sliced into bite-sized pieces
- 1 tbsp soy sauce + 1/2 tsp Worcestershire (sub for fish sauce)
- 2 green onions, cut into 2-inch pieces
- 1 cup fresh basil leaves (regular basil works great!)
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced

### Sauce
- 3 tbsp oyster sauce
- 3 tbsp soy sauce (sub for light + dark soy)
- 1/4 tsp molasses (optional - adds color like dark soy)
- 2 tsp sugar
- 1 tbsp water

## Instructions

1. **Prep noodles:** Soak rice stick noodles in hot water for 8-10 minutes until pliable but still slightly firm. Drain well.

2. **Mix sauce:** Combine oyster sauce, soy sauce, molasses (if using), sugar, and water in a small bowl.

3. **Heat wok:** Get your wok or large skillet VERY hot over high heat. Add oil.

4. **Aromatics:** Add garlic and jalapeños; cook 10 seconds until fragrant but not burnt.

5. **Onion:** Add onion and bell pepper; cook 1 minute until slightly softened.

6. **Chicken:** Add chicken and soy sauce mixture; stir-fry until cooked through, about 2 minutes.

7. **Noodles and sauce:** Add green onion, noodles, and sauce. Toss vigorously for 1 minute until sauce coats all the noodles evenly.

8. **Basil:** Remove from heat immediately. Add basil leaves and toss until just wilted.

9. **Serve:** Plate immediately while hot. Add extra sriracha if desired!

## Tips
- HIGH HEAT is essential - this cooks fast!
- For MAXIMUM spice: Use 5+ jalapeños with seeds, add sriracha on top
- Don't overcrowd the wok - cook in batches if needed
- Regular basil works perfectly - it wilts into the dish beautifully
