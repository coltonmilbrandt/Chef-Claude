# Spicy Ginger Garlic Chicken Stir-Fry

**Source:** [Garlic-Ginger Chicken Stir-Fry - Skinnytaste](https://www.skinnytaste.com/garlic-ginger-chicken-stir-fry/)
**Prep Time:** 10 min | **Cook Time:** 10 min | **Marinating:** 15 min | **Total:** 35 min
**Servings:** 4 (halve for 2)

## Description
A flavorful stir-fry with ginger, garlic, and chili paste that transforms chicken into a restaurant-quality dish. High protein and satisfying.

## Ingredients

### Sauce/Marinade
- 1 (1-inch) knob fresh ginger, minced
- 1/4 cup reduced sodium soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon sugar (or monk fruit for lower carb)
- 1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 1-2 teaspoons chili paste (to taste)

### Main
- 3/4 pound boneless chicken breast, thinly sliced into strips (for 2 servings)
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- 1/2 medium red onion, cut into chunks
- 3 cups baby bok choy, roughly chopped
- 2 tablespoons dry-roasted peanuts, crushed (optional garnish)
- Fresh basil, torn (optional garnish)

### For Serving
- Cauliflower rice

## Instructions

1. **Make marinade:** Whisk together ginger, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, sugar, sesame oil, cornstarch, and chili paste. Reserve 3 tablespoons for later.

2. **Marinate chicken:** Add chicken strips to remaining marinade. Let sit 15 minutes (up to 4 hours).

3. **Cook chicken:** Heat wok or skillet over high heat with 1 tsp oil. Cook chicken in batches, 2-3 minutes per side until golden. Transfer to plate.

4. **Saute onions:** Add remaining oil. Saute onions until lightly charred but still crisp (about 2 minutes).

5. **Combine:** Return chicken to skillet. Pour reserved marinade and 1/4 cup water into pan. Cook 1 minute.

6. **Add bok choy:** Stir in bok choy until wilted.

7. **Serve:** Transfer to plates over cauliflower rice. Top with crushed peanuts and torn basil.

## Tips
- Have all ingredients prepped before cooking - stir-fries go fast!
- Adjust chili paste for desired heat level
- Great meal prep option - chicken and sauce keep well
