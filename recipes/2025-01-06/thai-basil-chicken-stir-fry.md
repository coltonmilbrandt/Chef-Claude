# Thai Basil Chicken Stir Fry

**Source:** [Thai Basil Chicken - RecipeTin Eats](https://www.recipetineats.com/thai-basil-chicken-stir-fry/)
**Prep Time:** 10 min | **Cook Time:** 5 min | **Total:** 15 min
**Servings:** 2

## Description
A quick Thai stir-fry with bold basil flavor that tastes just as good as restaurant quality. Ready in just 15 minutes.

## Ingredients

### Main
- 225g (7oz) chicken thigh fillets, skinless and boneless, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1 green onion, cut into 4cm (2") lengths
- 1 cup Thai basil leaves, loosely packed
- 2 large garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 1 bird's eye or Thai chilli, deseeded and finely chopped
- 1.5 tbsp oil (peanut, vegetable, or canola)

### Sauce
- 2 tsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tsp sugar
- 2 tbsp water

### For Serving
- Cauliflower rice (for low-carb)

## Instructions

1. **Make sauce:** Combine all sauce ingredients in a small bowl.

2. **Heat wok:** Heat oil in wok or pan over high heat.

3. **Aromatics:** Add garlic and chilli; cook 10 seconds (avoid inhaling fumes).

4. **Cook chicken:** Add white parts of green onions and chicken; fry until cooked (approximately 2 minutes).

5. **Add sauce:** Pour in sauce and cook 1 minute until water reduces to create a thick glossy coating.

6. **Finish:** Toss in green onion tops and basil leaves; stir until just wilted.

7. **Serve:** Immediately over cauliflower rice.

## Tips
- Thai Basil has a hint of aniseed flavor; regular basil works as a substitute
- Finely chop garlic rather than mincing to prevent burning in the hot wok
- The sauce should coat the stir-fry without excess pooling
- Best served fresh immediately after cooking
