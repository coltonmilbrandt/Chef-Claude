# Mexican Chicken Bowl with Avocado

**Source:** [Chicken Burrito Bowl - Real Simple Good](https://realsimplegood.com/chicken-burrito-bowl/)
**Prep Time:** 5 min | **Cook Time:** 20 min | **Total:** 25 min
**Servings:** 4 (halve for 2)

## Description
A Paleo and Keto-friendly burrito bowl with seasoned chicken, creamy avocado, and cauliflower rice. Fresh, filling, and perfect for weight loss goals.

## Ingredients

### Seasoning
- 2 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 1/2 tsp ground pepper

### Chicken
- 1 tbsp avocado oil
- 3/4 lb boneless chicken thighs (for 2 servings)

### Bowl Components
- 3 cups spinach (or greens of choice)
- 2 tablespoons cilantro, chopped
- 1/4 red onion, finely diced
- 1 lime, cut into wedges
- 1 avocado, diced
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder
- 2 cups cauliflower rice
- 2 tablespoons salsa
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. **Season chicken:** Mix seasoning ingredients and coat chicken thighs on both sides.

2. **Cook chicken:** Heat skillet over medium heat with avocado oil. Cook chicken undisturbed for 5-6 minutes, flip, and cook another 5-6 minutes until cooked through. Remove and set aside.

3. **Prep toppings:** Prepare greens, cilantro, onion, and lime wedges.

4. **Make quick guac:** Combine diced avocado with some red onion and garlic powder. Mash lightly, squeeze lime juice, season with salt and pepper.

5. **Cook cauliflower rice:** In the same skillet, add a bit of oil and cook cauliflower rice with salsa for 3-5 minutes until warm.

6. **Assemble bowls:** Chop chicken into bite-sized pieces. Layer greens, cauliflower rice, chicken, remaining onion, cilantro, and mashed avocado. Serve with lime wedges.

## Tips
- Use the same pan for chicken and cauli rice to get extra flavor
- Great for meal prep - store components separately
- Add hot sauce or jalapeño for extra kick
