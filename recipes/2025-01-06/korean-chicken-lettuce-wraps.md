# Korean Chicken Lettuce Wraps

**Source:** [Quick Gochujang Lettuce Wraps - Gimme Some Oven](https://www.gimmesomeoven.com/gochujang-lettuce-wraps/)
**Prep Time:** 10 min | **Cook Time:** 10 min | **Total:** 20 min
**Servings:** 4 (halve for 2)

## Description
Spicy Korean-inspired lettuce wraps with gochujang sauce. Low-carb, high protein, and packed with flavor.

## Ingredients

### For the Chicken
- 1 pound boneless chicken thighs, sliced into bite-sized pieces
- Sea salt and freshly-cracked black pepper
- 1 tablespoon oil
- 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced or minced

### For the Gochujang Sauce
- 3 tablespoons gochujang paste
- 2 tablespoons low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon maple syrup (or honey)
- 1/2 teaspoon toasted sesame oil

### For the Wraps
- 8-10 butter lettuce leaves
- 1 cup thinly sliced English cucumber
- 1 cup shredded carrots
- Thinly-sliced green onions
- Toasted sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. **Make sauce:** Whisk together gochujang, soy sauce, maple syrup, and sesame oil in a small bowl. Adjust sweetness or spice as desired.

2. **Cook chicken:** Season chicken with salt and pepper. Heat oil in a large pan or wok over high heat. Add chicken and garlic, sauteing 4-5 minutes while stirring occasionally until browned and cooked through.

3. **Coat with sauce:** Remove from heat and stir in half the gochujang sauce until evenly coated.

4. **Assemble:** Fill each lettuce cup with saucy chicken, top with cucumbers, carrots, drizzle of remaining sauce, green onions, and sesame seeds.

5. **Serve:** Enjoy while warm.

## Tips
- Butter or Bibb lettuce works best for easy wrapping
- Can prep components ahead and assemble just before serving
- Add sriracha for extra heat
