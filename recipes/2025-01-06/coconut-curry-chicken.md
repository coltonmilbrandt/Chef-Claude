# Coconut Curry Chicken

**Source:** [Coconut Curry Chicken - Downshiftology](https://downshiftology.com/recipes/coconut-curry-chicken/)
**Prep Time:** 15 min | **Cook Time:** 15 min | **Total:** 30 min
**Servings:** 4 (halve for 2)

## Description
A simple one-pan meal featuring chicken in a creamy coconut sauce with warm curry spices. Dairy-free and delicious.

## Ingredients

### Main
- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
- 1/2 medium yellow onion, diced
- 1/2 red bell pepper, diced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated
- 1.5 boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cut into 1-inch cubes (~3/4 pound)

### Spices
- 1 tablespoon curry powder
- 3/4 teaspoon garam masala
- 1/2 teaspoon ground coriander
- Kosher salt and black pepper to taste

### Sauce & Serving
- 1 (13.5-ounce) can full-fat coconut milk
- 1/2 lime, juiced (~1 tablespoon)
- Cauliflower rice (for low-carb)
- 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro, chopped

## Instructions

1. **Saute aromatics:** Heat oil in a large saute pan over medium heat. Add onion and bell pepper; saute 2-3 minutes. Stir in garlic and ginger for another minute.

2. **Cook chicken:** Add cubed chicken with curry powder, garam masala, coriander, salt, and pepper. Saute 5-7 minutes until chicken is cooked through, stirring frequently.

3. **Add coconut milk:** Pour in coconut milk and lime juice. Simmer 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until flavors meld.

4. **Serve:** Spoon curry over cauliflower rice and garnish with fresh cilantro.

## Tips
- Store curry and rice separately for up to 4-5 days
- Can freeze up to 3 months in freezer-safe containers
- Great for meal prep
